Privacy Policy

Effective: November 27, 2026 · Operator: Hyoungdon Kim · Contact: lynofind@gmail.com

LYNO Find is an app that finds the photos and documents on your phone. There is no sign-up, the operator runs no server, and the operator receives no personal information from you. Reading and searching your photos and documents all happens on your device.

1. Information processed only on your device

InformationWhy it is used
Photos you allow the app to accessSearch by the text in photos and by what photos show (similar photos, finding photos by words). Photo details such as the date taken and location are also stored in the index.
Documents you pick or connect (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV)Search by document title and content
What you say during voice searchTurning speech into a search query. Only the device's own speech recognition is used, and audio is not stored.
App settings and lock settingsRunning the app. Kept in the device's secure storage.

The search index is encrypted and stored only on your device. The AI models that understand photo content are built into the app and run on your device without sending photos anywhere. While your phone is charging, the app can keep indexing new photos little by little even when it is closed; this also happens only on your device.

2. Information sent outside the device

The operator does not receive your photos, documents, text found in photos, search queries or voice. The app uses no usage statistics or analytics tools. The app connects to the internet only for the ads described below, for checking for a new version, and for Google Drive if you connect it yourself (section 6).

New version check: when you open the app, it checks once whether a new version is available. On Android, the Google Play app performs the check. On iPhone, the app sends only its identifier (com.lynofind.app) and your region to the Apple App Store and receives the latest version number. No photos, documents, text found in photos, queries, voice or information that identifies you is sent. As with any internet connection, Apple and Google may receive your device's IP address.

Ask AI: only when you tap this button in the app guide, the app or web page of the AI you chose (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) opens. A question asking how to use that feature and the address of the LYNO Find guide are filled in for you (for Gemini, the question is copied for you to paste). The question never includes your photos, documents, text found in photos, search queries or voice. From then on, that AI service's own policy applies.

3. Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads: a banner under search results, a small ad card on the Photos and Documents tabs, and a short rewarded ad before deleting in "Photos to delete" (none for the first deletion after installing). To show ads and prevent fraudulent clicks, Google may process:

  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as an app-specific device identifier
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks
  • Error and diagnostic information about the ad feature

LYNO Find does not use personalized ads, and the app does not ask for permission to track you. Ad requests never include your photos, documents, text found in photos, queries or voice. Where consent is required, Google's consent screen appears before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy settings" in the app's settings. Search keeps working even if ads fail to load. LYNO Find Pro (coming soon) shows no ads.

Google's processing follows the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. When you share something yourself

If you pick a photo or document in the search results and tap "Share", the original is passed to the app you choose, and that app's policy applies from then on. The app never sends photos or documents on its own.

6. Google Drive data (Pro, coming soon)

This feature is planned for LYNO Find Pro. If you choose to connect your Google account, you can search the files in your Google Drive together with the photos and documents on your phone. You can disconnect at any time, and the rest of the app works without connecting.

Data we access (read-only scope drive.readonly)

DataWhy it is used
The list of files in your DriveFinding the files to search
File names and metadata such as type, size, modified date and folderShowing search results and re-indexing only files that changed
The content of files downloaded for search or that you selectSearching by the text and content inside files, downloading a file you choose to your phone, and passing it to the share sheet

LYNO Find never creates, edits or deletes files in your Google Drive, and never changes their sharing settings. It requests read-only access only.

Where data is processed and stored

  • All processing happens only on your phone. Google Drive data goes directly from Google's servers to your phone and never passes through any server of ours. The operator runs no server.
  • The only thing kept on your phone is a search index built from file metadata and content. It is encrypted and stored only on your device.
  • Files downloaded for search or sharing are kept briefly in temporary storage on your phone and deleted when the task finishes. The originals stay in your Google Drive.
  • Your Google sign-in token is kept only in your phone's secure storage. The operator never receives or sees your Google password.

Sharing: when you pick a file in the search results and tap "Share", the file is downloaded to your phone and passed to the app you choose, and that app's policy applies from then on. The app never sends your Google Drive data to any other person or company on its own.

What we never do

  • No human reads your data. No one, including the operator, can see your Google Drive data; with no server, there is no way to.
  • No advertising. Google Drive is offered only in Pro, which has no ads, and Google Drive data is never used for ad selection, personalized ads or retargeting.
  • No transfer to third parties. We do not sell it or give it to data brokers, advertising platforms or information resellers, and we do not use it for lending or credit decisions.
  • No AI model training. The AI model used for search is a finished model built into the app. Google Drive data is never used to train or improve this or any other model.

Disconnecting and deletion

  • When you tap "Disconnect Google Drive" in the app's settings, the Google Drive index, any temporary downloaded files and the sign-in token on your phone are deleted, and Google access is revoked.
  • Deleting the app also deletes this data.
  • You can also remove LYNO Find's access at any time in your Google Account third-party access settings. The app then deletes the Google Drive index on your phone the next time it opens.

Compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy: LYNO Find's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

The index and settings the app creates stay only on your device. You can delete them with "Delete search index" or "Delete all local data" in the app's settings, and they are also removed when you delete the app. Search queries and recognized speech are not stored. The app never changes your original photos and documents. It deletes an original only when you select photos and tap delete, or swipe a document and confirm. Photos go to the phone's trash (Recently Deleted on iPhone); documents are deleted right away. Deletion of Google Drive data follows section 6.

8. Children

LYNO Find is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email lynofind@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with its effective date. The Korean version of this policy is the original.
