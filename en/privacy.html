Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Operator: Hyoungdon Kim · Contact: lynofind@gmail.com

LYNO Find is an app that finds the photos and documents on your phone. There is no sign-up, the operator runs no server, and the operator receives no personal information from you. Reading and searching your photos and documents all happens on your device.

1. Information processed only on your device

InformationWhy it is used
Photos you allow the app to accessSearch by the text in photos and by what photos show (similar photos, finding photos by words). Photo details such as the date taken and location are also stored in the index.
Documents you pick or connect (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV)Search by document title and content
What you say during voice searchTurning speech into a search query. Only the device's own speech recognition is used, and audio is not stored.
App settings and lock settingsRunning the app. Kept in the device's secure storage.

The search index is encrypted and stored only on your device. The AI models that understand photo content are built into the app and run on your device without sending photos anywhere. While your phone is charging, the app can keep indexing new photos little by little even when it is closed; this also happens only on your device.

2. Information sent outside the device

The operator does not receive your photos, documents, text found in photos, search queries or voice. The app uses no usage statistics or analytics tools. The app connects to the internet only for the ads described below and for checking for a new version.

New version check: when you open the app, it checks once whether a new version is available. On Android, the Google Play app performs the check. On iPhone, the app sends only its identifier (com.lynofind.app) and your region to the Apple App Store and receives the latest version number. No photos, documents, text found in photos, queries, voice or information that identifies you is sent. As with any internet connection, Apple and Google may receive your device's IP address.

3. Ads

A Google AdMob banner ad appears at the bottom of the app screen. To show ads and prevent fraudulent clicks, Google may process:

  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as an app-specific device identifier
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and clicks
  • Error and diagnostic information about the ad feature

LYNO Find does not use personalized ads, and the app does not ask for permission to track you. Ad requests never include your photos, documents, text found in photos, queries or voice. Where consent is required, Google's consent screen appears before the first ad, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy settings" in the app's settings. Search keeps working even if ads fail to load.

Google's processing follows the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. When you share something yourself

If you pick a photo or document in the search results and tap "Share", the original is passed to the app you choose, and that app's policy applies from then on. The app never sends photos or documents on its own.

5. Permissions

The app reads only the documents and folders you pick yourself.

6. Retention and deletion

The index and settings the app creates stay only on your device. You can delete them with "Delete search index" or "Delete all local data" in the app's settings, and they are also removed when you delete the app. Search queries and recognized speech are not stored. The app never changes or deletes your original photos and documents.

7. Children

LYNO Find is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email lynofind@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with its effective date. The Korean version of this policy is the original.
