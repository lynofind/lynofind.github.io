Using smart folders

1. What are smart folders?2. Which folders are there?3. How do I use them?4. Nothing is moved

1. What are smart folders?

LYNO Find sorts your photos into folders by itself. New photos go into the right folders automatically.

2. Which folders are there?

On from the start: Screenshots, Selfies, Favorites, By place, Trips, Receipts, Notes, Food, Pets

Turn on as you like: 38 folders in 8 groups: Basics, Places & trips, Photos with text, People & animals, Events, Hobbies & sports, Things & food, Nature & scenery

3. How do I use them?

1. Tap the Photos tab below.

2. Under Smart folders, tap "See all" to open the folders.

3. In "Choose folders", open a group and turn folders on or off.

4. Turn on the first row of a group, like "All Hobbies & sports", to gather the whole group in one folder.

4. Nothing is moved

Photos are never copied or moved. The originals stay where they are; folders only gather them for you. All sorting happens on your phone.
