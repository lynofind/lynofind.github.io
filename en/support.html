Help

If you have a question or a problem, email lynofind@gmail.com. You do not need to send your original photos or documents.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos or documents sent to a server?

No. Reading text in photos, understanding photo content, reading documents, search and speech recognition all happen on your device. Photos, documents, queries and voice never leave the device.

The first indexing takes a long time.

At first the app reads your photos one by one to prepare them for search. Keeping the app open is fastest. When the app is closed, it continues little by little while your phone is charging and not in use.

I allowed access to "Selected Photos" only.

Only the selected photos can be searched. To search all photos, change LYNO Find's photo access to "All Photos" in your device settings.

How do I add documents?

On the Documents tab, tap "Connect folder" to search the documents in that folder; new documents added there are picked up automatically. You can also add files one at a time with "Pick documents". Supported formats are PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT and CSV.

On Android, I can't select the Download folder.

Android does not allow connecting the Download folder itself. Create a new folder inside Download, move your documents there and connect that folder, or add files directly with "Pick documents".

Voice search doesn't work.

Voice search uses only your phone's on-device speech recognition so that your voice never leaves the device. On phones without on-device speech recognition (on Android it requires Android 12 or later), voice search is turned off. On supported phones, allow the microphone permission the first time; you can check it under LYNO Find in your device settings.

Are my photos or queries used for ads?

No. The banner ad at the bottom of the screen is not personalized, and ad requests never include your photos, documents or queries. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I want to delete the index and app data.

Use "Delete search index" or "Delete all local data" in the app's settings. Deleting the app also removes everything the app created. Queries are never stored in the first place. Your original photos and documents are never changed or deleted.

I want to lock the app.

In settings, you can turn on a PIN lock or biometric lock (Face ID or Touch ID on iPhone, fingerprint or face unlock on Android).
